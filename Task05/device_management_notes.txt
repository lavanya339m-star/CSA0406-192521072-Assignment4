Device-management evidence checklist:
1. Identify block devices: lsblk -o NAME,TYPE,SIZE,FSTYPE,MOUNTPOINTS
2. Inspect scheduler: cat /sys/block/<disk>/queue/scheduler
3. Inspect mount and free space: findmnt -T . ; df -h .
Interpretation:
- Drivers translate generic I/O requests to controller commands.
- Buffering absorbs speed differences; caching reuses hot data.
- Spooling queues work for serial devices.
- The block layer exposes addressable blocks to filesystems.
